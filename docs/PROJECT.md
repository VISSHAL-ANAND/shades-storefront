# Project Overview

## Business
Shades is a men's fashion and lifestyle retail store in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Supplied Instagram screenshots show apparel such as shirts, tees, trousers, sweatshirts, streetwear, and accessories including watches, sunglasses, and caps. The live catalogue must come from the client.

## Product
A premium customer-facing storefront connecting online product discovery and purchasing with visits to the physical store.

## Audience and geography
Initial market: Coimbatore. Whether delivery is limited to Coimbatore or broader shipping is not yet confirmed.

## Design direction
Cinematic editorial fashion presentation inspired by the supplied CANALI reference. Preserve Shades' identity; do not copy another brand.

## Scope
Included direction: responsive storefront, campaign/hero presentation, rolling clothing showcase, product listing/detail experiences, cart, online checkout, store information, directions, and contact.

Separate scope unless approved: custom admin dashboard, POS integration, advanced inventory synchronization, loyalty systems, and complex logistics integrations.

## Commercial status
Proposal-stage project. Product assets and operational data are expected after agreement. Do not fabricate them.
