# Product Requirements (Initial)

## Goal
Create a premium cinematic storefront that makes Shades' products discoverable and purchasable while encouraging visits to the physical store.

## Primary journeys
1. Visitor lands on the cinematic homepage and understands the brand.
2. Visitor explores the rolling showcase or navigates to a category.
3. Visitor browses products and opens a detail page.
4. Visitor selects an available variant and adds it to cart.
5. Visitor checks out and receives order confirmation.
6. Visitor finds store information and plans a physical visit.

## Functional requirements
- Responsive navigation and homepage.
- Campaign hero using client-approved media.
- Rolling product showcase with seamless motion and accessible fallback.
- Catalogue with categories and brand labels where supplied.
- Product details with approved images, descriptions, prices, sizes, and variants.
- Cart and checkout.
- Payment integration; provider and flow to be finalized.
- Store information, directions, and WhatsApp contact.

## Non-functional requirements
- Mobile-first usability.
- Optimized media and good Core Web Vitals.
- Keyboard navigation, focus states, contrast, and reduced-motion support.
- Secure handling of customer and payment data.
- Clear loading, empty, error, and unavailable-stock states.

## Client questions
- Product catalogue and brand-asset permissions.
- Delivery geography, shipping charges, local delivery, and pickup.
- Inventory source and stock update process.
- Returns, refunds, cancellations, and exchanges.
- Payment account ownership and settlement.
- Customer account requirements and GST invoice requirements.
- Domain, hosting, maintenance, and commercial terms.
