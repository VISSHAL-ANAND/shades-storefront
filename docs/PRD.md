# Product Requirements

## Goal
Build a premium, cinematic and mobile-first Shades Coimbatore storefront that makes products discoverable, supports a coherent shopping journey and encourages physical store visits.

## Frontend journeys implemented as preview
1. Land on the editorial homepage.
2. Explore category tiles and the continuous rolling clothing showcase.
3. Browse the catalogue, filter categories and sort products.
4. Open a product, select size/colour/quantity and add it to the bag.
5. Review the bag, update quantities and remove items.
6. Complete a clearly labelled checkout form demonstration.
7. Search the catalogue and view the editorial/about page.
8. Find the store information placeholder.

## Required production capabilities
- Approved campaign media and product photography.
- Authoritative product data, variants, prices, SKU and stock.
- Server-side cart/order and payment integration.
- Confirmed delivery, pickup, shipping, tax and returns rules.
- Order confirmation and customer communication.
- Verified store address, opening hours and contact details.

## Non-functional requirements
Responsive mobile-first layout; keyboard operability; visible focus; adequate contrast; reduced-motion support; optimized media; stable layout; secure server-side handling of personal and payment data; clear loading, empty, error and unavailable-stock states.

## Client inputs still required
Catalogue and media permissions; shipping geography and charges; inventory source; returns/refunds/cancellations/exchanges; payment account and provider; GST invoice rules; customer account requirements; store address/hours/contact; hosting/domain/maintenance.
